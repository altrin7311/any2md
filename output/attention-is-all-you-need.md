# Attention Is All You Need

> [!summary] TL;DR
> The [[Transformer]] model, based solely on attention mechanisms, outperforms complex recurrent and convolutional models in [[machine translation]] tasks while being more parallelizable and requiring less time to train.

## Key Points

- Proposes a new simple network architecture, the [[Transformer]]
- Eliminates recurrence and convolutions entirely
- Achieves superior quality in [[machine translation]] tasks
- Requires significantly less time to train
- Establishes a new single-model state-of-the-art [[BLEU score]] for English-to-French translation
